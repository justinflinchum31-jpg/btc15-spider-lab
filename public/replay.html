EXPLANATION OF RESEARCH RULE

How the sample directions were derived

UP

EMA 9 exceeded EMA 21, and the closing price had increased over the previous five minutes.

DOWN

EMA 9 was below EMA 21, and the closing price had decreased over the previous five minutes.

ABSTAIN

If the two rules did not agree, no directional position was counted. Each available outcome was measured 15 minutes later, without overlapping windows.

Rule is a simple untrained diagnostic baseline, not the v2.4 multi-signal model. The published data uses non-overlapping 15-minute windows starting after 45 minutes of indicator history. This archive contains 5,902 consecutive BRTI one-minute candles collected from Oct 5–9, 2026, of 10,080 originally requested. VWAP, order-book snapshots, trading costs, fills, and contract settlement records are not available; no executable net P&L can be computed.
